Meet the future of Tech

Gulli 1 (Performance Beast)

The Gulli 1 is built for competitive gamers who demand power and precision. With a 13th Gen Intel Core i9 CPU and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 GPU, it delivers smooth, high-FPS gameplay in modern AAA titles. The 165Hz FHD+ display ensures ultra-responsive visuals, while the advanced cooling system keeps performance consistent during intense sessions.

Gulli 2 (Budget-Friendly Warrior)

The Gulli 2 strikes the perfect balance between affordability and performance. Equipped with an Intel Core i7 and RTX 3050, it’s great for casual and mid-tier gamers. Its bold design, responsive 144Hz display, and upgraded thermals make it a reliable choice for gamers who want solid specs without breaking the bank.

Gulli 3 (Premium Experience)

The Gulli 3 is a premium gaming machine crafted for those who want luxury, performance, and futuristic aesthetics. Featuring an Intel Core i9, NVIDIA RTX 4080 GPU, and a vibrant 240Hz QHD+ display, it’s designed for immersive gameplay and creative workflows. The RGB-lit chassis, ultra-thin build, and high-fidelity speakers complete the elite experience.
